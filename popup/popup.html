    Privacy

    GBP Sphere has no servers of its own. It reads public profile data from Google pages you open and keeps a short cache in your browser. Only when you click: the speed check sends the website address to Google PageSpeed Insights, the keyword finder asks Google for search suggestions, and the category tools download Google's public category list from GitHub. Nothing else leaves your browser.
